# Natural Earth country boundaries

- Publisher: Natural Earth; public domain.
- Catalog: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/
- Exact downloaded upstream: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
- Retrieved: 2026-10-30.
- The downloaded GeoJSON is archived unchanged. The build script selects the Americas except Canada, USA, Greenland and Bermuda, and transforms longitude/latitude into small SVG paths for the Latin America cost map. No remote runtime dependency.
- This is small-scale illustrative cartography, not an authoritative statement about sovereignty. Small islands may be absent at 1:110m. Uncovered tax countries stay gray; no rate is imputed from neighbors.
