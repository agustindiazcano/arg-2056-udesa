# arXiv monthly submissions

- Publisher: arXiv.
- Source page: https://arxiv.org/stats/monthly_submissions
- Download: https://arxiv.org/stats/get_monthly_submissions
- Retrieved: 2026-10-30.
- Original CSV is archived unchanged. `submissions` counts new submissions, not versions, citations or accepted peer-reviewed journal articles. Historical counts can be revised by arXiv.
- `node scripts/build_ai_evidence.mjs` sums twelve monthly observations for each completed year 2020–2025 into `web/src/tour/aiResearchCounts.json`. The incomplete 2026 year is excluded. No inference about AI authorship or causal productivity is made from these counts.
